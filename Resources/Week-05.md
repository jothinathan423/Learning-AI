# Week 5 Resources — Error Analysis & Traces

Curated further reading for complete traces, sampling discipline, open coding, error taxonomies,
and prioritizing fixes by frequency × severity.

## Beginner

- **Hamel Husain's blog posts on "error analysis" and "eval-driven development" for LLM apps**
  (Blog) — one of the most widely cited independent practitioner voices on exactly this week's
  topic; written specifically for teams shipping LLM products, not academic researchers.
- **LangSmith's / Langfuse's documentation on "traces"** (Official Docs) — vendor documentation
  for what a complete trace actually captures in a real observability tool, grounding Topic 01
  in a concrete product.
- **"How to Debug LLM Applications" style engineering blog posts from AI infrastructure vendors**
  (Blog) — practical, example-driven write-ups of the inspection-then-categorize workflow this
  week teaches.

## Intermediate

- **"Basics of Qualitative Research" (Corbin & Strauss)** (Book) — the foundational qualitative-
  research text that open coding (Topic 03) is borrowed from; the original methodology behind a
  technique now used in ML error analysis.
- **"Content Analysis: An Introduction to Its Methodology" (Krippendorff)** (Book) — a standard
  reference for building a coding taxonomy from raw qualitative notes, directly applicable to
  Topic 04's error taxonomy construction.
- **Eugene Yan's blog posts on evaluating and improving LLM applications** (Blog) — practitioner
  writeups that connect error analysis directly to eval-set construction, bridging into Week 6.

## Advanced

- **"A Grounded Theory Approach" — original grounded theory literature (Glaser & Strauss, 1967)**
  (Book) — the deep methodological root of open coding and category-building, for readers who
  want the qualitative-research theory in full rather than its ML-adapted summary.
  no fabricated URL — searchable by title/author.
- **Academic HCI/error-analysis papers on "failure mode taxonomies" in applied ML systems**
  (Research Paper) — broader ML-systems literature on categorizing production failures by
  frequency and impact, generalizing Topic 05 beyond LLM apps specifically.
- **Internal engineering blog posts from AI product teams (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI, or applied-AI
  startups) describing their own error-analysis or "vibe check" processes** (Blog) — first-hand
  accounts of Topic 06–08's discipline (choosing a fix target, writing a prediction first)
  applied at real scale.
